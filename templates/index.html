    Diseases We Detect

    Early Blight

    Caused by Alternaria solani. Dark concentric lesions on older leaves.

    Late Blight

    Caused by Phytophthora infestans. Water-soaked spots that spread rapidly.

    Yellow Leaf Curl

    Viral disease transmitted by whiteflies causing leaf curling and yellowing.

    Mosaic Virus

    Causes mottled light and dark green pattern on leaves reducing yield.

    Healthy

    No disease detected. Plant shows normal growth and development patterns.
